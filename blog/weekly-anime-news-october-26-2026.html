THE BIG SCREEN

Kaiju No. 8 names its final mission

The anime’s concluding chapter is a film, Kaiju No. 8: The Final Mission, announced for 2027. Production I.G continues the animation, with Shigeyuki Miya directing. The announcement came with a short teaser visual video, while a release date and international theatrical plans were not specified in the source.

Source: Crunchyroll News · Kaiju No. 8 official account

TRAILER DESK

Four new looks at what’s coming

Date A Live F Last Date

A new trailer and visual set the film’s Japanese theatrical opening for March 26, 2027. The official trailer is from KADOKAWAanime; no international release date was included in the announcement.

Source: Crunchyroll News · Official video: KADOKAWAanime

Future GPX Cyber Formula returns for its 35th anniversary

Sunrise released a main trailer for a double feature: an HD-remastered compilation film and the newly produced short Future GPX Cyber Formula ASURADA. Both are scheduled to screen in Japan from February 19, 2027. The short continues the story after the SIN OVA; no overseas screening plan was announced.

Source: Crunchyroll News · Cyber Formula official website

ELECEED sets an April 2027 broadcast

The official anime site confirmed an April 2027 Japanese broadcast and shared an English-language version of the trailer. Crunchyroll says it will stream the supernatural action series when it airs. The webtoon follows Jiwoo, whose catlike reflexes make him a secret rescuer, and Kayden, a powerful agent trapped in the body of a cat.

Source: Crunchyroll News · Official ELECEED trailer

MURCIÉLAGO reveals its main cast

The January 2027 adaptation of Yoshimurakana’s action manga has a teaser video, visual, and three lead cast announcements: Hitomi Ueda as Kuroko Koumori, Minori Suzuki as Hinako Tozakura, and Sumire Uesaka as Chiyo Yanaoka. HIDIVE says it will stream the show in the U.S., Canada, U.K., Australia, and New Zealand.

Source: Anime News Network · Official video: NBCUniversal Anime/Music

MANGA DESK

A new manga from Shinobu Ohtaka

Geekrown, also referred to as Geek and Crown, begins on Kodansha’s Magazine Pocket app on October 27. The Magi and Orient creator’s new story centers on a military officer caught in a succession crisis. Anime News Network reported that K MANGA plans to add it on October 26. Check the apps for local availability and any regional access conditions.

Sources: ANN: launch announcement · ANN: K MANGA availability

Oricon’s Japan chart: Detective Conan takes the top spot

Detective Conan Volume 109 debuted at No. 1 on Oricon’s weekly manga ranking dated October 26, with an estimated 114,169 copies sold in Japan during October 12–18. A separate special edition placed No. 6. That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime Volume 33 ranked No. 2, while Laid-Back Camp Volume 19 moved up to No. 3. These are reported Japan sales estimates, not a global chart.

Source: Oricon Japan Anime News
