THE CAT AND THE DRAGON SEASON 2 IS ANNOUNCED

The anime is returning for a second season in 2027, with Crunchyroll set to stream the continuation.

The first season aired from July to September 2026. Crunchyroll describes the series as a story about an orphaned dragon raised by magic-wielding cats. This is an announcement recap, not a review or a claim about audience response.

The official anime website and accounts announced on September 19 that Season 2 of The Cat and the Dragon is in production and slated to air in 2027. Crunchyroll says it will stream the season when it premieres. The report gave no more specific premiere date.
