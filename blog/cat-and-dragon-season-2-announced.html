THE CAT AND THE DRAGON SEASON 2 IS ANNOUNCED

The anime is returning for a second season in 2027, with Crunchyroll set to stream the continuation.

A NEW CHAPTER FOR THE FANTASY ADVENTURE

The Cat and the Dragon is officially returning for a second season. The announcement follows the end of the first season and confirms that the fantasy series will continue in 2027, giving viewers another chapter to look forward to rather than treating the first run as a one-season story.

The first season aired for 12 episodes from July through September 2026. Crunchyroll has confirmed that it will stream the second season when it premieres in Japan, although a more specific premiere date has not been announced yet.

The series follows a fire-breathing dragon raised by a family of magic-wielding cats. That unusual family bond is at the heart of the story, and the second-season announcement keeps the focus on the warm, bittersweet fantasy premise that made the anime stand out.

WHY IT MATTERS

The announcement also gives the series a clear place in the next wave of fantasy anime conversations. A second season means the first season’s setup has room to breathe: relationships can deepen, the rules of the world can become clearer, and the contrast between a small companion and a huge mythical creature can grow into something emotionally richer.

Season announcements are often the first clear signal that a series has earned enough audience interest to continue. For fans, the important takeaway is simple: this is not a one-season goodbye. The Cat and the Dragon has more story ahead, and the 2027 return gives the community a new date to watch.
