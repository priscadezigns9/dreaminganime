Solo Leveling: Beyond the System

Crunchyroll announced at Anime Expo 2026 that Solo Leveling: Beyond the System is a theatrical film continuing Sung Jinwoo’s story after Season 2. The announcement names Crunchyroll, Aniplex, Netmarble, D&C Media and Kakao Piccoma as co-producers, with A-1 Pictures returning. No release date was announced in the report. The previous copy incorrectly described Season 2 as an upcoming July 2026 release; Season 2 aired in Winter 2025.

Source: Crunchyroll News

Corrections

Claims about “Cinematic Fluidity” pipelines, neural-linked lighting, frame-data tracking, and a July release for Solo Leveling Season 2 were unsupported or incorrect. They have been removed. No animation pipeline or production assessment is asserted here.
