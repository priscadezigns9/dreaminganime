Solo Leveling: What Comes After Season 2?

Arise from the Shadow has already aired. The next confirmed chapter is Solo Leveling: Beyond the System, a theatrical movie announced by Aniplex and Crunchyroll. Here’s what’s official—and what fans are still waiting to learn.

Season 2 · Winter 2025Movie · In developmentRelease date · TBA
Quick answer: Season 2 finished airing in Winter 2025. A theatrical continuation, Solo Leveling: Beyond the System, was announced in July 2026 and is in development. The announcement did not include a release date.

Season 2 has already aired

Solo Leveling Season 2 -Arise from the Shadow- followed the first season and aired in Winter 2025. Crunchyroll’s pre-premiere update set its January 4, 2025 premiere and named the ending song “UN-APEX” by TK from Ling tosite sigure. In short: this page is a status update, not a countdown to Season 2.

The next chapter is a theatrical movie

At Anime Expo 2026, Aniplex and Crunchyroll announced Solo Leveling: Beyond the System. The official report describes it as a theatrical movie adapting the next phase of Sung Jinwoo’s journey and continuing from where Season 2 left off. It also confirms Crunchyroll is co-producing alongside Aniplex, Netmarble, D&C Media and Kakao Piccoma, with A-1 Pictures returning from the series.

The announcement included a teaser key visual and concept video presented by English voice actor Aleks Le. It confirmed development—not a theatrical date, production schedule, or regional rollout plan.
