RE:ZERO SEASON 4: OFFICIAL RELEASE DETAILS

Re:ZERO Season 4 began on April 8, 2026. Crunchyroll’s published schedule divided the season into two arcs: the 11-episode Loss Arc, followed by the eight-episode Recapture Arc, which began August 12. The dated information below comes from publisher coverage and the official anime announcements.

What was confirmed

Season 4 is directed by Masahiro Shinohara at WHITE FOX. The official announcement named Masahiro Yokotani for series composition, Haruka Sagawa for character design and Kenichiro Suehiro for music. Those are credited production details, not a basis for claims about unannounced staff expansion or animation quality.

The season’s opening song, “Recollect,” is performed by Konomi Suzuki featuring Ashnikko, with production by Giga and TeddyLoid. The ending theme, “Ender Ember,” is performed by MYTH & ROID featuring TK from Ling tosite sigure.

Earlier versions of this post repeated rumors about a doubled staff, claimed access to leaked storyboards, and assigned numerical animation-accuracy scores. Those statements were not supported by the cited official sources and have been removed. This page summarizes confirmed release and credit information; it is not a review of the episodes.
