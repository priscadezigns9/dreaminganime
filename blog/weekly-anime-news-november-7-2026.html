This Week in Anime

A new adaptation is headed for December, familiar favorites are lining up 2027 returns, and Dragon Ball Super: Beerus is getting a fresh dubbed rollout. Here are the week’s notable announcements, with original reporting, official art and videos linked.

NEW ADAPTATION

Spirit Circle anime set to stream in December

On November 5, the official announcement revealed a TV anime adaptation of Satoshi Mizukami’s Spirit Circle, with global streaming scheduled for December 2026. The teaser introduced its central past-lives conflict, and the announcement named the main cast and staff. Streaming platforms have not yet been specified.

Official teaser visual via Crunchyroll News and the anime’s official announcement.

Sources: Crunchyroll News · Official anime site · Official teaser by It’s Anime / REMOW

RETURNING FAVORITE

The Dangers in My Heart Season 3 gets an April 2027 window

On November 5, a new teaser and visual announced the third season’s Japanese broadcast for April 2027. Kyotaro and Anna’s relationship moves forward, while the official teaser hints at a new chapter for the pair.

Season 3 announcement image via Crunchyroll News.

Sources: Crunchyroll News · Official series news

COZY CAMPING RETURNS

Laid-Back Camp Season 4 arrives in April 2027

The fourth season’s April 2027 premiere was announced with a new trailer and key visual. The trailer also previews the opening song “Brand New” by Asaka, ending song “Happa no Tegami” by Eri Sasaki, and insert song “Tonari ni Ireba” by Kimi no Ne.

Season 4 key visual via Crunchyroll News and the official series account.

Sources: Crunchyroll News · Official series announcement

MANGA ADAPTATION

Rainy Day Serenade reveals cast and key art

On November 6, the anime adaptation of Haruka Kawachi’s manga revealed its main staff, cast, teaser trailer and key art. The series is scheduled to begin broadcasting in Japan in April 2027. Yume Miyamoto voices Hina Katagiri and Takeo Otsuka voices Takaaki Hongo.

Official key art via Crunchyroll News and the anime’s official account.

Sources: Crunchyroll News · Official anime announcement

JANUARY PREMIERE

BLESS names its premiere date

A new trailer released November 7 set BLESS to begin airing in Japan on January 8, 2027. It previews the ending theme “New Page” by HIKKA. Crunchyroll says it will stream the series as it airs.

Trailer image via Crunchyroll News; see the linked article for the embedded official trailer.

Sources: Crunchyroll News · Official anime announcement

FANTASY SERIES

Gacha Girls Corps sets a January 7 start

Following a November 7 event in Japan, the official site revealed a January 7, 2027 Japanese TV start date, a new main visual and two cast additions: Aimi Ozawa as Migle and Renon Esumi as Lennon. Crunchyroll says it will stream the fantasy series as it airs.

Official main visual via Crunchyroll News and the anime’s official announcement.

Sources: Crunchyroll News · Official anime site

DUB NEWS

Dragon Ball Super: Beerus dubs stream November 12

Crunchyroll announced English, Spanish and Portuguese dubs for Dragon Ball Super: Beerus, starting November 12 at 11 a.m. Eastern, with new dubbed episodes arriving weekly. The English trailer is below; the returning cast includes Sean Schemmel as Goku, Christopher R. Sabat as Vegeta, Jason Douglas as Beerus and Ian Sinclair as Whis.

Sources: Anime News Network · Official dub trailer by Crunchyroll
