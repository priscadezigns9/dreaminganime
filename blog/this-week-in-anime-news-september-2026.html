THIS WEEK IN ANIME NEWS

September’s latest headlines are moving in three directions at once: Netflix is preparing the next stage of a major manga adaptation, Crunchyroll is building out its fall English-dub slate, and Kojima Productions is expanding the cast and creative signal around PHYSINT. Here are the stories worth keeping on your radar this week.

STEEL BALL RUN RETURNS SEPTEMBER 25

Netflix Tudum confirmed that the 2nd STAGE of STEEL BALL RUN JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure begins streaming on September 25. New episodes are scheduled weekly on Fridays, with 11 episodes planned for this stage. The new trailer introduces Mountain Tim, Hot Pants, and Funny Valentine as Johnny Joestar and Gyro Zeppeli enter the dangerous “Devil’s Palm” desert crossing.

Source: Netflix Tudum — STEEL BALL RUN 2nd STAGE

CRUNCHYROLL’S FALL DUB SLATE EXPANDS

Crunchyroll published its fall 2026 English-dub lineup on September 15. The announced slate includes The Apothecary Diaries Season 3, Black Clover Season 2, Magic Knight Rayearth, Overgeared, Aoashi Season 2, and several more series. The announcement gives English-speaking viewers an early guide to the dubbed titles arriving with the new season, while cast and release timing details continue to be added.

Source: Crunchyroll News — Fall 2026 English Dubs

PHYSINT ADDS BILL SKARSGÅRD

Anime News Network reports that Hideo Kojima revealed Bill Skarsgård as the lead actor for PHYSINT, Kojima Productions’ planned action-espionage game. The update also covered continuing progress on casting, talent scans, and collaborations with outside creators. Although it is a game rather than an anime series, PHYSINT belongs in this week’s anime-adjacent watchlist because it sits directly at the intersection of cinematic storytelling, performance capture, and Japanese pop-culture production.

Source: Anime News Network — PHYSINT casting update

THE SIGNAL

This week’s pattern is clear: established franchises are entering new release stages while platforms compete to make the fall season easier to follow across subtitles, dubs, streaming schedules, and interactive media. For Anime Dreamers, the practical watchlist is simple—mark September 25 for Steel Ball Run, check Crunchyroll’s regional dub schedule, and keep PHYSINT on the radar for its next production reveal.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR DREAMING ANIME

The wider lesson from this week is that a useful anime website must do more than collect attractive trailers. It must explain what each announcement is, when it arrives, where viewers can find it, and whether the link being presented is official. Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 points toward a major fall streaming event on October 20. Fool Night gives the calendar a November 26 global Netflix premiere and introduces a premise that deserves careful editorial attention. LEGO ONE PIECE lands earlier on September 29 as a two-part special with a family-friendly franchise hook. Each title deserves a different editorial treatment, but all three belong in the same release and availability system. The trailer hub should preserve the official video source. The release tracker should distinguish series, seasons, and specials. The legal directory should store region, platform, language details, source URL, and verification date. The blog should provide the context that a database cannot provide, including why the visual direction matters and what kind of viewer may respond to the project. This is the model Dreaming Anime is beginning to build: editorial voice on top, verified navigation underneath, and an audience community that is always directed toward legitimate platforms. The current trailer rotation keeps Gachiakuta Season 2 at the front of the site, but this week’s reports create three strong candidates for future Cinema features and title-guide entries. The next step is not to chase every announcement indiscriminately. It is to build a reliable rhythm where important signals are researched, labeled, connected, and made useful. That is how Dreaming Anime can grow from a stylish anime hub into a trusted guide for what to watch next.
