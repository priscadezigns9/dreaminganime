This Week in Anime

Re:ZERO is returning for a fifth season, WITCH WATCH is set for 2027, and a wave of new trailers is giving us a first look at what’s next. Here are the week’s confirmed highlights, plus affordable anime collectibles on Amazon.

Global anime coverage · Dreaming Anime

November 9–15, 2026Verified sourcesOfficial updates
SEASON 5 CONFIRMED

Re:ZERO Season 5 enters production

The series’ official accounts announced a fifth season during the 10th anniversary event in Japan on November 14, alongside a teaser visual featuring Subaru and Rem. Release timing and further staff details have not been announced yet, so for now it’s a green light—not a premiere date.

Official teaser visual released with the Season 5 announcement.

Sources: Crunchyroll News

RETURNING FAVORITE

WITCH WATCH Season 2 set for 2027

The official series account confirmed that Season 2 will air in 2027 and unveiled a new teaser visual. The announcement followed the manga’s finale in Weekly Shonen Jump on November 15. No more specific premiere window was given in the report.

The new visual accompanied the 2027 broadcast announcement.

Sources: Crunchyroll News

NEW ADAPTATION

Living It Up in Another World Until I Get Bored gets a TV anime

The light-novel series has a TV anime in the works for January 2027 broadcast and streaming, according to the announcement reported November 13. Cast and production staff details were still to come at the time of reporting.

The newly announced adaptation is scheduled for January 2027.

Sources: Crunchyroll News

NEW ANIME · JANUARY 6

Multi-Mind Mayhem reveals trailer, music and added cast

A new trailer set the Japanese TV start for January 6, 2027. The update also named six additional cast members and the theme songs: “Isekai” by Maverick Mom for the opening, and “Brand New Day” by Tokino Sora for the ending.

The November 13 update includes the new trailer, a key visual and additional cast.

Sources: Crunchyroll News · Anime Trending

FIRST TEASER TRAILER

Nabe ni Tama o Ukenagara cooks up a January 2027 debut

The first teaser for the food-travel manga adaptation revealed a January 2027 start, lead cast and staff. Juntaro’s female form is voiced by Shu Uchida, while Masaaki Mizunaka voices the character’s older-man form.

Crunchyroll’s November 12 report summarizes the trailer and newly announced cast.

Sources: Crunchyroll News

MOVIE TRAILER + RELEASE DATE

ghost – end of night unveils main trailer

The original anime film released a main trailer featuring “life” by Hitsujibungaku, a new key visual and additional cast. Director Shingo Natsume’s film is scheduled to open in Japan on February 11, 2027.

The film’s official trailer and key visual arrived November 10.

FALL SIMULCAST TRAILER

A Certain Dark Item drops a “VS ITEM” trailer

The new trailer spotlights a conflict between two ITEM teams in Academy City. The series is already airing in Japan and is part of Crunchyroll’s Fall 2026 simulcast lineup.

Crunchyroll’s November 13 report links the trailer and confirms its simulcast status.

Sources: Crunchyroll News

ANOTHER 2027 RETURN

Gushing Over Magical Girls Season 2 confirms 2027

A birthday update for protagonist Magia Baiser brought a new “ultra teaser” visual and confirmation that Season 2 will broadcast in 2027. The post did not give a more precise release date.

New visual and 2027 broadcast window announced November 14.

Sources: Crunchyroll News

FILM ANNOUNCEMENT

Little Shark’s Day Out gets a second theatrical film

A second feature-length film is in development under the title Eiga Odekake Kozame: Mizu no Machi no Omoide (“Memories of Water Town”). The announcement arrived with a teaser trailer and visual; no release date has been revealed.

Official announcement with teaser on November 15.

Sources: Crunchyroll News · Official anime site

NEW MERCH REVEALS

Bandai previews new SH Figuarts anime figures

Bandai Namco’s Tamashii Nations 2026 showcase revealed figures in development for Kagurabachi, Demon Slayer, Hunter × Hunter and One Piece. The announced designs include Chihiro Rokuhira, Mitsuri Kanroji and Muichiro Tokito, Gon from the Chimera Ant arc, and One Piece characters Franky, Jinbe and Kuzan. These are reveals, not current Amazon deals; release dates and prices were not announced.
