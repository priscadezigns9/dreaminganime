THE BIG NARUTO REVEALS

At the October 10, 2026 “NARUTO: What’s Next?” panel at New York Comic Con, the franchise announced a new Kishimoto-drawn manga, a four-episode Naruto x Ninku TV anime, and updates under Project Rasengan. This recap follows the official Naruto announcements and Crunchyroll’s panel report. Any later dates or production details should be checked against those sources.

NARUTO SHADOW RISING

The new manga, titled Naruto Shadow Rising, was announced with a teaser. Story details have not yet been disclosed. Crunchyroll and the official Naruto site said more information is expected at the Naruto stage at Jump Festa 2027, scheduled for December 19–20, 2026.

Naruto Official Site: NYCC roundup

NARUTO x NINKU: FOUR EPISODES

The official announcement confirms a four-episode TV limited series. Masashi Kishimoto and Ninku creator Koji Kiriyama supervise the project; Noriyuki Abe directs at Studio Pierrot, with Tetsuya Nishio as chief animation director and character designer and Nozomi Fukui as assistant director. No release date was announced in the October 10 report. The official synopsis says Kakashi sends Team 7 on a mission, appoints Naruto acting leader, and the trio encounters Fūsuke after becoming lost in another world.

PROJECT RASENGAN AND OTHER ANNOUNCEMENTS

Project Rasengan describes a mid- to long-term franchise roadmap across manga, anime, cards and games, with projects teased through 2029. The official materials highlighted an international release plan for Naruto Mobile, with more information expected at Jump Festa, and a worldwide Naruto Card Game launch in June 2027. The card-game announcement specifies three starter decks and a first booster set.

Crunchyroll also reported an anime adaptation of the Dr. STONE “TERRAFORMING” epilogue chapter in production, without a confirmed format or release date. Its NYCC streaming slate lists Berserk of Gluttony Season 2 and The Remarried Empress for 2027, and announces The Boxer without a release date. Consult the linked announcements for territories and later changes.

WHAT IS STILL UNKNOWN

The Naruto x Ninku premiere date remains unannounced, and further story information for Shadow Rising is pending. The other listed adaptations also have limited details. This page separates those open questions from the confirmed episode count, credits and game announcements.
