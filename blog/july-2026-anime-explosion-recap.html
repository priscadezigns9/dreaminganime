BLACK CLOVER SEASON 2: OCTOBER BROADCAST ANNOUNCED

Crunchyroll News reported that the official anime website announced an October 2026 broadcast for Black Clover Season 2. Ayataka Tanemura is returning as director, Studio Pierrot is producing the animation, and the report names additional staff. The report did not confirm a split-cour format, an episode count, or claims about a production-budget increase. Those details are not included here.

SOLO LEVELING: BEYOND THE SYSTEM

At Anime Expo 2026, Aniplex and Crunchyroll announced Solo Leveling: Beyond the System as a theatrical film continuing from the end of Season 2. Crunchyroll reports that A-1 Pictures will return and that the film is in development. No release date or theatrical rollout schedule was announced in that report.

Read Crunchyroll’s movie announcement.
