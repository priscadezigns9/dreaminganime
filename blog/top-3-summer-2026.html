Three Summer 2026 Anime Worth Revisiting

Summer 2026 has passed, so this is a look back—not a preview or a ranking. These three series were listed in Crunchyroll’s published summer lineup and offer notably different starting points: supernatural action, historical strategy, and long-form fantasy.

Editorial scope: Release and studio details below are drawn from the linked official lineup. The short descriptions are editorial context, not claims about personal viewing or a measured popularity ranking.

BLACK TORCH

Crunchyroll’s Summer 2026 lineup lists 100studio as the studio and describes the premise as reluctant ninja Jiro meeting feline Rago, bringing human and spectral worlds together.

The hook pairs a familiar ninja frame with a supernatural companion. That gives readers a clear entry point: look for how the series balances action with the relationship between Jiro and Rago, rather than judging it only by its fight scenes.

Jaadugar: A Witch in Mongolia

The official lineup lists Science SARU and describes Sitara using her knowledge against the Mongolian Empire after the invasion of her homeland.

Its historical setting and emphasis on knowledge set it apart from the season’s more combat-forward premises. It is a useful choice for viewers looking for political and cultural context alongside a character-led story.

Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation — Season 3

Crunchyroll lists Studio Bind and frames the season around Rudeus entering a new chapter with powerful foes, hard choices, and personal growth.

As a continuing season, it asks returning viewers to bring the history of earlier arcs with them. Its appeal is not a fresh-start premise; it is the accumulated consequences attached to a long-running fantasy story.
