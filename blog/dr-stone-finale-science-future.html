DR. STONE SCIENCE FUTURE: FINALE DETAILS

The final episode of Dr. STONE SCIENCE FUTURE, and the anime adaptation as a whole, aired June 25, 2026, according to Crunchyroll’s pre-finale report. The official series account previewed Senku’s final confrontation with Why-man and announced voice actors Koichi Yamadera and Kotono Mitsuishi for the role.

This is a brief finale notice, not a scene-by-scene review. Earlier copy claimed Mimi had audited the episode’s chemistry and assigned it a “100 billion percent” satisfaction score. Those were not verifiable observations, so they have been removed.
