SEASON 1 REVIEW

Komi Can’t Communicate

Komi’s goal is concrete: make one hundred friends, one conversation at a time. That modest structure lets each attempt to connect matter amid a classroom full of outsized personalities. The comedy comes from the gap between how classmates interpret Komi and what she is trying to express, while the friendships make progress feel collective.

Romantic Killer

Anzu would rather spend time with her favorite interests than follow a romance script imposed by a game-like system. Makeine: Too Many Losing Heroines!

The season is interested in what happens after rejection, when the presumed lead has to keep living among the people and routines left behind.
