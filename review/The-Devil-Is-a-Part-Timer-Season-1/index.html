The Devil Is a Part-Timer! Season 1

A demon lord stranded in modern Tokyo takes a part-time fast-food job while his old enemy follows him into an unfamiliar world.

QUICK TAKE

The Devil Is a Part-Timer! Season 1 finds comic mileage in an ambitious demon lord learning how rent, shifts, and customer service work. The contrast between fantasy stakes and ordinary employment is the core joke, while the returning hero adds an uneasy connection to the old conflict. The season is a light workplace fantasy with violence, suggestive imagery, and profanity in its advisory.

FROM DEMON LORD TO SHIFT WORKER

After being forced out of his world, the demon lord Satan lands in modern Tokyo with limited magic and even less money. Working at a fast-food restaurant becomes a practical route to survival, and he takes the job with the same ambition he once brought to conquest.

Emi Yusa, the hero who pursued him, also arrives in Japan, giving the series a second perspective on the old conflict. The two must contend with everyday life while neither can quite forget who the other was.

THE COMEDY OF ORDINARY LIFE

Season 1’s strongest joke is how seriously Satan treats a shift, a paycheck, and workplace advancement. Fantasy power collides with practical constraints, and the series finds warmth in its characters’ attempts to make a life in a place where they are strangers.

The workplace and apartment scenes create a relaxed rhythm between supernatural incidents. The humour is approachable, but the series occasionally leans on suggestive imagery and slapstick, so it is not entirely free of mature content.

THE FIRST SEASON ONLY

This review covers the original 2013 season, not the later continuation. It works as a workplace comedy with a fantasy premise, and viewers looking for a complete resolution to every character arc should know this is one stage of a larger franchise.

Crunchyroll marks it not recommended under 14 and lists violence, sexualized imagery, and profanity. Its official listing is useful for series information, but current streaming access may differ in Trinidad and Tobago.

VERDICT

The Devil Is a Part-Timer! Season 1 makes the everyday details of employment funny by giving them to an ex-demon lord. Its fantasy-meets-workplace premise and unlikely character dynamic are charming, even if the story remains one part of a larger series.

Content note: Not recommended for viewers under 14; the advisory lists violence, sexualized imagery, and profanity.
