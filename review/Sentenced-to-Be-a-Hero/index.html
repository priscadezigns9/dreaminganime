EDITORIAL REVIEW

WHY IT WORKS

Sentenced to Be a Hero begins from a severe premise in which heroism is punishment. Xylo Forbartz, described as a condemned goddess killer, fights monstrous abominations inside a world that treats duty as a sentence. The concept creates room for dark fantasy, sacrifice, and questions about what makes a hero.
