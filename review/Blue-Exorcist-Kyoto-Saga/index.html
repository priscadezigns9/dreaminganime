BLUE EXORCIST: KYOTO SAGA / SERIES REVIEW

Blue Exorcist: Kyoto Saga

Rin and his fellow exorcists confront a crisis in Kyoto that tests trust inside the Order and pushes the story into a new regional conflict.

← BACK TO REVIEWS
SPOILER-LIGHT REVIEW · The 2017 Kyoto Saga only. This is a continuation of Blue Exorcist, not a review of the 2011 original season or later arcs.
QUICK TAKE

Kyoto Saga gives Blue Exorcist a focused continuation centered on the Kyoto branch of the Exorcist Order, a dangerous relic, and the strain placed on Rin’s relationships. It is most rewarding for viewers who know the setup and characters from the original series. The arc combines supernatural action with conflict about trust, identity, and family.

A CONTINUATION, NOT A RESTART

Blue Exorcist: Kyoto Saga picks up after the events that lead the Exorcist Order to investigate a crisis in Kyoto. Rin’s demonic heritage remains a source of fear and tension, and his friends’ responses to him shape the emotional stakes alongside the supernatural threat.

The Kyoto setting gives the arc a distinct atmosphere and a new group of characters within the Order. The story’s conflict is not only about defeating an enemy; it is also about deciding who can be trusted when fear and suspicion have already taken root.

TEAMWORK UNDER STRAIN

The ensemble gives the season a useful structure: characters with different backgrounds and skills must work together even when old doubts remain. Rin’s stubborn energy keeps the action moving, while the consequences of the earlier revelations prevent the group dynamic from becoming friction-free.

Supernatural battles and the relic-centered danger offer spectacle, but the more engaging moments are those that connect the action to personal loyalties. Viewers new to the franchise may find the relationships and context arrive too quickly without the original season.

WATCH ORDER AND AVAILABILITY

This review covers the 2017 Kyoto Saga only. It is best approached as a continuation after the 2011 Blue Exorcist season; later arcs are not included. The official site provides Kyoto Saga-specific introduction material.

Crunchyroll lists violence, supernatural horror, and profanity among the series descriptors, but no clear minimum age advisory was confirmed specifically for this scope. The available Amazon Blu-ray found was Region B/2 and is unsuitable for the Americas, so no incompatible disc is recommended here.

VERDICT

Kyoto Saga builds on Blue Exorcist’s central family and trust conflicts while moving the action to a new setting and crisis. It is a worthwhile continuation for established viewers, but it is not the best first entry and the region-incompatible disc listing is intentionally omitted.
