Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon?

SERIES REVIEW · SPOILER-LIGHT
QUICK TAKE

Behind the cheeky title is a surprisingly durable fantasy adventure: Bell Cranel’s growth, the gods’ rival Familias, and Orario’s Dungeon give the series a strong engine. Its best arcs make the city feel alive; fan-service, familiar harem beats, and uneven pacing are recurring drawbacks.

Dreaming Anime is a Trinidad-rooted, Caribbean anime publication for fans worldwide.

A TITLE THAT MISLEADS ON PURPOSE

Crunchyroll’s official synopsis starts with a playful question: Bell wants to find romance in Orario, but his first encounters with the Dungeon leave him in the damsel-in-distress role. That contrast gives the opening its joke, but the series’ staying power comes from Bell’s earnest effort to become a stronger adventurer and from the people who help him grow.

The title may suggest a one-note harem comedy. The wider story is more interested in the social and mythic machinery around the Dungeon: adventurers, gods, and competing Familias all have something to gain from Bell’s progress. The strongest stretches turn that setup into a genuine fantasy adventure rather than a string of romantic misunderstandings.

ORARIO IS THE REAL HOOK

The city’s structure gives the long-running series room to shift between dungeon exploration, rivalries, and character-focused arcs. Bell’s initial weakness makes growth readable, while Hestia’s small Familia gives his victories a personal scale before the stakes widen. The relationships are at their best when they reveal a character’s values, not just who might fall for Bell.

That breadth is also the series’ strength across multiple seasons. It can move from light comedy to danger and emotional pressure without abandoning its central question: what does it take for a novice adventurer to earn a name in a city built around the Dungeon?

FAN-SERVICE AND PACING ARE THE TRADE-OFF

The comedy and romantic framing are part of the identity, but suggestive scenes and familiar harem conventions can pull focus from the adventure. Crunchyroll’s own advisory flags sexualized imagery, violence, and suggestive dialogue. Pacing also varies: some stretches build tension around the Dungeon and the cast, while others feel more episodic or convenient.

When the series puts character choices ahead of easy escalation, it has real momentum. When it leans too hard on fan-service or repeated misunderstanding, its richer world feels temporarily sidelined.

VERDICT

Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon? grows beyond its title into a warm, expansive fantasy adventure with a clear central hero and a setting that can sustain many seasons. It is not equally focused throughout, and the fan-service may not suit everyone, but the combination of dungeon progression, gods, and an earnest lead is compelling for viewers who enjoy long-form fantasy.

Viewer note: Crunchyroll marks the series as not recommended for minors under 14 and lists sexualized imagery, violence, and suggestive dialogue. Its series page currently says videos are not available; streaming access varies by region.
