BLACK TORCH / EPISODE 6

A slower training episode reframes Rago’s past and the conflict around him.

TRAINING THAT OPENS OLD WOUNDS

Episode 6 slows the team’s forward rush with defense training against hypnosis and hallucinations. The exercise becomes a frame for the squad’s past: Reiji’s family fracture, Ichika’s earlier grief, and Rago’s long history. That makes this less an action chapter than a deliberate pause to explain why these characters carry so much distrust into the same room.

Rago’s account is the episode’s most meaningful turn. Once revered locally, he was approached by the mountain god Amagi to join the fight against humans hunting mononoke. Rago’s memory complicates the Bureau’s version of him as a rampaging threat: he refused to be recruited into Amagi’s cause and chose to seal himself away. That choice gives Rago a past shaped by loyalty and refusal, not simply destructive power.

WHAT THE BACKSTORIES ADD

Reiji’s memories add context to his discipline and the scar that has followed him; Ichika’s earlier history stays present as the team’s losses come into view. Jiro takes less of the spotlight, but his questions help connect Rago’s memories to the conflict the team is facing now. The character material makes the group feel less like a roster of abilities and more like people with incompatible histories.

WHERE THE PACE SLIPS

The episode delivers a lot of history in a short stretch, so the training and revelations sometimes feel like a fast briefing rather than a discovery. Rago’s true form is a striking reveal, though its conspicuously different 3D look may pull some viewers out of the surrounding 2D action. The slower pace is useful for character context, but it also pauses the immediate plot momentum.

VERDICT

Episode 6 is a backstory chapter first and an action episode second. Its strongest contribution is re-framing Rago’s past and letting the other team members’ wounds register; the trade-off is less time for Jiro’s own perspective and for the mission to advance. It works as a pause that deepens the conflict, provided the next episode turns these revelations into choices and consequences.
