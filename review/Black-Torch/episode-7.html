BLACK TORCH / EPISODE 7

“3 on Three” brings the team back into the field—and puts Rago’s power in a new light.

← BACK TO BLACK TORCH
EPISODE REVIEW · OCTOBER 2026
Episode 7 spoilers ahead. This review discusses the mononoke mist mission and character revelations.
QUICK TAKE

The mist mission restores Jiro’s animal-talk ability, while Rago’s unleashed strength and Reiji’s family conflict raise the stakes.

THE TEAM GETS BACK IN THE FIELD

Episode 7, “3 on Three,” sends the Black Torch unit to investigate a city swallowed by mononoke mist. The mission gives Jiro a chance to use his animal-communication gift again: he moves with the quickness of a shinobi and gathers information from animals beyond Rago. That return matters because it reconnects the story to the unusual ability that first set Jiro apart.

The mist also gives the episode a stronger action frame. Rago’s restraint no longer limits his full power, and the sudden shift changes the balance of the fight. Rather than treat that power-up as a clean victory, the episode uses it to make Rago’s strength feel consequential—and to leave the question of how Jiro and Rago manage it hanging over the team.

REIJI’S PAST ENTERS THE FIGHT

The confrontation becomes more personal when Reiji’s brother appears. The reveal turns a mission against unfamiliar mononoke into a family conflict with unfinished history, while Reiji’s own past gives his composure a more vulnerable edge. The episode is strongest when it lets that history change the way the characters face the danger, rather than pausing only to explain it.

WHAT WORKS—AND WHAT STILL NEEDS ROOM

Jiro’s animal-talk ability finally has a practical role in the investigation, and the action has more movement than the previous backstory-focused episode. Rago’s power is an effective shock, but the story now needs to clarify its cost and limits. Reiji’s brother is a strong hook; the episode introduces the conflict faster than it can resolve it, so the next chapter will need to give their history space.

VERDICT

Episode 7 feels like a return to forward motion. The citywide mist gives the team a concrete problem, Jiro’s animal communication becomes useful again, and Reiji’s family history turns the mission personal. Rago’s power shift is the episode’s biggest spark, but the most satisfying payoff will depend on what it demands from him and Jiro next.
