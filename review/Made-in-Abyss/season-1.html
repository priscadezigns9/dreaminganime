BEAUTY AT THE EDGE OF THE ABYSS

← ALL ANIME REVIEWS
Review scope: Made in Abyss television anime, Season 1 · Episodes 1–13. This review ends with the first 13-episode TV season and excludes the Dawn of the Deep Soul film and later seasons.
Crunchyroll content advisory: Not recommended for minors under sixteen; Violence, Torture. Availability and ratings can vary by region.

A WORLD THAT INVITES DESCENT

At the edge of an immense, mysterious chasm, young cave-raider Riko meets Reg, a boy-like robot, and the two set out toward the Abyss. The official series description presents the journey as a search for its mysteries; the setting itself is the show’s great organizing idea, with each deeper layer suggesting a new environment and a new cost.

WONDER WITH A SHARP EDGE

Season 1 uses the descent as its narrative framework: each step into the unknown expands the setting and raises the stakes. The official synopsis connects Riko's search with questions about the Abyss and Reg's identity, giving the premise a clear source of forward motion. Crunchyroll's content advisory, which names violence and torture, signals that danger is central to this expedition rather than a detachable element.

FINAL VERDICT

Made in Abyss Season 1 is best approached as mystery-driven expedition fantasy in which wonder is closely tied to danger. This review ends with the 13-episode first TV season, before the Dawn of the Deep Soul film and later season. Crunchyroll's advisory explicitly flags violence and torture; take that warning into account when deciding whether to watch.
