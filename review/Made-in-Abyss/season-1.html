Made in Abyss: Season 1

A breathtaking descent turns wonder into a question of cost.

← ALL ANIME REVIEWS
Review scope: Made in Abyss television anime, Season 1 · Episodes 1–13. This review ends with the first 13-episode TV season and excludes the Dawn of the Deep Soul film and later seasons.
SPOILER-LIGHT · SEASON 1 · 13 EPISODES
QUICK TAKE

Made in Abyss builds its first season around a powerful visual idea: every step downward reveals a new world and makes the return journey more daunting. That sense of discovery is compelling, but the series pairs it with extreme peril and disturbing material, so its content warning belongs at the center of the recommendation.

THE WORLD HAS VERTICAL MOMENTUM

The Abyss gives the season a map that is also a narrative engine. New layers promise unfamiliar environments and unanswered questions, while descent itself makes progress feel irreversible. The setting creates curiosity before the characters even meet the next danger.

WONDER IS NEVER A SAFETY PROMISE

Riko and Reg’s partnership provides the journey with a human point of view, but the contrast between youthful curiosity and the world’s brutality is deliberate. Beautiful discoveries do not soften the threats; they make the gap between fascination and safety more unsettling.

A STRONG FIT WITH A SERIOUS CAVEAT

The first season suits viewers drawn to exploratory fantasy, intricate setting design, and a mystery that opens outward. Its depictions of violence and torture can be upsetting, and the platform advisory reflects a meaningful concern rather than a routine label.

VERDICT

Made in Abyss Season 1 is a striking expedition fantasy whose worldbuilding and sense of descent give it a distinctive shape. Its wonder is inseparable from danger, and the series can be extremely upsetting; viewers should take the listed advisory seriously. This review ends with the 13-episode first season.

Content note: Not recommended for minors under sixteen; Violence, Torture. Availability and ratings can vary by region.
