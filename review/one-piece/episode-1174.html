SAVE THE CHILDREN!

Episode 1174 gives the five-episode sequence its clearest emotional rallying point. The official title, “Save the Children! The Elbaph Warriors Rise Up,” places protection at the centre of the action. Dorry and Brogy return to the battlefield, and Scopper Gaban arrives with the Giant Warrior Pirates to help Elbaf fight. Those arrivals matter because they turn memory into action. Legendary figures are no longer only names from the past; they are choosing what to do in the present. The children make the conflict emotionally direct, and the episode understands that spectacle becomes more meaningful when the audience knows who is in danger. This is not only a contest between powerful groups. It is a test of whether the strongest people will use their strength responsibly. Episode 1174 is the most openly heroic chapter of the five, but it avoids feeling simple because the wider mystery remains unresolved. The result is a strong temporary victory that increases anticipation for what comes next.

FINAL VERDICT

This episode works best as part of the connected Elbaph sequence. It advances the conflict, adds emotional pressure, and leaves the next chapter with a clear question for the Straw Hats and the people of Elbaf.
