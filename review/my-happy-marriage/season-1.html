Season 1 review

THE FOUNDATION · 2023

Season-by-season review

Season 1 builds its romance through safety, patience, and the slow recovery of Miyo’s self-worth. Her arrival in Kiyoka’s household creates a careful contrast between neglect and trust, while the supernatural elements turn inherited power and family pressure into emotional stakes.

The season’s measured pacing gives small gestures room to matter: a boundary respected, a fear spoken aloud, or a person choosing to believe that kindness can be real. Kiyoka works because he is not presented as a magical solution to Miyo’s past; his care becomes convincing through consistency and restraint.

Verdict: Season 1 is a graceful foundation that earns its warmth by allowing trust to grow rather than forcing a shortcut.
