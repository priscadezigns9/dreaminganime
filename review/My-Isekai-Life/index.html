My Isekai Life: I Gained a Second Character Class and Became the Strongest Sage in the World!

A quiet salaryman enters a fantasy world and discovers that his monster-taming skills are only the beginning.

← BACK TO REVIEWS
SPOILER-LIGHT · FULL SERIES REVIEW · 2022 TV SEASON
QUICK TAKE

My Isekai Life builds its power fantasy around an unusually self-contained hero. Yuji Sano arrives in another world with monster-taming abilities, gains a second class, and becomes much stronger than his low-key manner suggests. The season’s most distinctive companions are the slimes that gather around him; the central tension is whether the world can make that overpowered premise feel like a journey rather than a checklist.

YUJI’S NEW LIFE STARTS WITH A SECOND CLASS

Yuji’s former life as an overworked employee gives way to a fantasy world where taming monsters becomes the doorway to greater power. His second class as a sage changes what he can do and how other people respond to him. Rather than making him a loud, self-celebrating hero, the series keeps Yuji comparatively quiet and focused, which makes the gap between his understated personality and exceptional abilities part of the appeal.

THE SLIMES GIVE THE POWER FANTASY A FACE

Yuji’s slimes are more than a convenient source of magical knowledge: they give the story its warmest recurring presence. Their curiosity and loyalty soften a plot that could otherwise become a sequence of skill upgrades. The Monster Tamer premise also gives Yuji a practical reason to rely on companions, even when his own abilities threaten to solve most problems alone.

A STRAIGHTFORWARD ADVENTURE, NOT A CHARACTER STUDY

The show is clearest when it leans into the satisfaction of discovering a new ability, facing a dangerous opponent, or letting Yuji’s quiet competence surprise people around him. It is less interested in closely unpacking the cost of his previous overwork or the emotional adjustment of starting over. The result is an easygoing fantasy power trip with a sympathetic center, rather than a deep exploration of its isekai premise.

THE TRADE-OFF OF AN OVERPOWERED LEAD

Yuji’s strength is part of the fantasy, but it can also flatten suspense: the question is often how quickly he will reveal another solution, not whether he can survive. The supporting cast and the slimes provide welcome texture, though the season’s straightforward storytelling will suit viewers who want an uncomplicated adventure more than those seeking intricate politics or a sharply evolving protagonist.

VERDICT

My Isekai Life is an accessible, comfortable fantasy for viewers who enjoy capable heroes, magical companions, and steady demonstrations of new powers. Yuji’s low-key demeanor and the slimes’ charm keep its overpowered premise from feeling entirely generic, even when the stakes are easy to read. It is a solid pick for a relaxed isekai watch, with modest ambitions beyond the pleasure of seeing Yuji and his companions handle the next challenge.
