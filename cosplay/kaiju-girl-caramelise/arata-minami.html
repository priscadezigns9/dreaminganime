The silhouette

Polished school style

Keep the overall look neat and approachable. Arata’s appeal is not a complicated costume; it is the confident, put-together presence of the popular student.

  • Clean dark school blazer or fitted jacket
  • Light shirt with a simple tie
  • Dark trousers and clean shoes
  • Neat, natural-looking hair styling

Build checklist

01 · Clothing

Start with the uniform

Use a dark blazer, white or pale shirt, and a restrained tie. Choose breathable fabrics for long convention hours.

02 · Hair

Keep it natural

Style the hair with a soft, controlled shape rather than an exaggerated spike. A wig or temporary colour is easy to maintain.

03 · Details

Make the polish visible

Pressed clothing, tidy cuffs, clean shoes, and subtle grooming do more for the character than oversized accessories.

04 · Performance

Play the contrast

Use relaxed confidence in public scenes, then let the expression soften in quieter moments. Arata reads through timing and attention.
