One Piece / Loki

A practical, source-conscious build guide for the Accursed Prince of Elbaf — designed around silhouette, comfort, and safe convention wear.

Read character profile

Build the silhouette first

Loki’s scale is part of the character. Start with a strong stance, broad shoulder line, layered garments, and deliberate height rather than chasing an exact prop before the base fit works. The goal is a recognizable inspired build; do not present handmade pieces as official merchandise.

Base clothing

  • Dark, comfortable base layer for movement.
  • Structured outer layer or tunic with room for sitting and walking.
  • Layered belts, wraps, or trim added after the mobility test.

Texture & finish

  • Use fabric weight to suggest a giant warrior-prince.
  • Choose contrast edging that reads from a distance.
  • Test every attachment against stairs, doors, and crowds.

Color planning

Build from a dark foundation with controlled warm accents. Photograph the outfit in daylight and indoor venue lighting before finalizing trim.

Props

Make props from lightweight foam, cardboard, or other convention-approved materials. Round corners, cap points, and keep everything transportable. Never bring a sharpened, metal, or functional weapon. Check the event’s current prop policy before attending; venue rules override this guide.

Wig & makeup

  • Use a heat-safe synthetic wig and style in small sections; secure the hairline with pins or a wig cap.
  • Prioritize visibility and ventilation over extreme height.
  • Patch-test makeup and adhesives. Bring remover, water, a compact comb, and a repair kit.
  • Take breaks if heat, weight, or restricted vision becomes uncomfortable.

Sizing & fit

Measure chest, waist, hip, shoulder width, inseam, and head circumference while wearing the underlayers you will actually use. Compare measurements to the maker’s chart, not a generic S/M/L label. Leave room for sitting, breathing, and reaching. For commissions, request a measurement checklist and a written alteration policy.

Safety checklist

  • Check the convention’s prop, footwear, photography, and accessibility rules.
  • Keep sightlines clear; use a handler for large builds.
  • Secure loose straps and avoid trailing fabric near escalators.
  • Carry ID, water, medication, emergency contacts, and a repair kit.
  • Respect photographers, staff, and other attendees; ask before touching costumes or props.
