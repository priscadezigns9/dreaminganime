BUILD THE AZUMA LOOK

A practical guide for recreating Shino Azuma’s school-uniform look from You and I Are Polar Opposites, with comfort, fit, and character detail in mind.

SILHOUETTE AND PALETTE

Start with the school look

Build the recognizable foundation first: a neat school blazer, shirt, skirt, socks, and comfortable shoes. Keep the lines clean and the fit natural so the character reads clearly without becoming uncomfortable.

Keep the contrast controlled

Use the reference artwork to match the main dark, light, and accent tones. Avoid adding unrelated accessories that compete with the character’s simple, everyday silhouette.

FIT AND MATERIALS

Measure before ordering

Record bust, waist, hips, shoulder width, sleeve length, height, and shoe size. Compare those measurements with each seller’s chart instead of relying on S, M, or L.

Dress for a full convention day

Choose breathable fabric, soft underlayers, secure fasteners, and shoes you can walk in. Test sitting, bending, walking, and carrying a bag before the event.

HAIR, MAKEUP, AND EXPRESSION

Keep the styling natural and character-focused. Use a wig cap, detangle from the ends, shape gradually, and patch-test makeup before the event. Practice three expressions: composed, amused, and emotionally open. The expression is what turns a school uniform into an Azuma cosplay.

CONVENTION CHECKLIST

  • Outfit tested while walking and sitting
  • Wig secured and makeup patch-tested

  • Small repair kit, pins, tape, water, and charger
