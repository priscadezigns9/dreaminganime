Build the silhouette of the reborn mage from Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation.

The look

Make the mage

Start with the long dark coat, layered travel clothing, sturdy boots, and a staff or wand silhouette. Prioritize comfortable movement and secure construction so the costume works for a full convention day.

Use textured fabrics and restrained earth tones for the traveller feel. Add small details through pouches, straps, trim, and weathering rather than relying on fragile accessories.

Safe build checklist
  • Use lightweight foam or sealed cardboard for props.
  • Round and cover all hard edges.
  • Keep staff pieces collapsible for transport.
  • Test the outfit while walking, sitting, and carrying a bag.
