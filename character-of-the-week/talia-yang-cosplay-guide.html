Character of the Week · Cosplay guide

Talia Yang

A reference-led build plan for Talia’s revealed Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 look. Keep the pink hair, dark face covering, angular black-and-gray jacket, and chrome-like arm detail true to the official image; adapt the fit for your comfort.

Start with one source

Choose a Talia reference

Before sourcing fabric or shaping props, open the official character material and choose one full-body Talia image to follow. The visible cast portrait shows a short, asymmetrical pink cut with a long segmented side extension, a dark face covering, a cropped dark jacket with geometric gray panels and pink-purple accents, and a chrome-like forearm detail. Keep your color placement and proportions tied to that image instead of blending in generic cyberpunk accessories.

For lower-body details that are not clear in the cast portrait, use the full official visual as the authority rather than guessing the exact shoes or adding unconfirmed gear.

The silhouette

Build the recognizable pieces

01 · Hair

Pink cut and side extension

Start with a pale pink, short asymmetrical wig. Add the long segmented side extension shown in the official visual; secure it to a wig cap or hidden support so it will not swing into your face.

02 · Face

Dark face covering

Use a lightweight, breathable mask that echoes the dark respirator shape. Make it removable and keep your peripheral vision and airflow clear; do not attach rigid pieces near the eyes.

03 · Jacket

Angular cropped techwear

Choose a dark cropped jacket as the base. Build the gray geometric shoulder and sleeve panels from flexible fabric appliqués, then add the visible magenta-purple trim from your chosen reference.

04 · Arm detail

Lightweight chrome accent

Recreate the cybernetic forearm with foam, fabric, or flexible thermoplastic. Keep the wrist and fingers movable, edges rounded, and the prop light enough for a full convention day.

05 · Lower half

Follow the full-body reference

Use the dark trousers and pale seam details visible in the official image as cues. The cast portrait does not clearly establish exact footwear, so choose comfortable closed shoes and avoid calling them screen-accurate.

06 · Coverage

Adapt the base layer

The reference has an open, cropped silhouette. A fitted black tank or bodysuit underneath keeps the visual balance while offering more coverage and easier movement.

Build order

A practical cosplay checklist

Before sewing

Save one official full-body reference, note the main color blocks, and measure the jacket and trousers. Do a quick paper layout before cutting any fabric.

Keep the mask and arm piece detachable. Test the wig extension with the jacket collar so they do not catch on each other.

Before the event

Walk, sit, raise both arms, and check your view through the mask. Pack spare fasteners, wig pins, water, and a small repair kit.

Check venue rules for face coverings and props. Use no sharp edges, functioning weapons, or heavy metal pieces.
