Johnny Joestar

Johnny Joestar is the central racer of STEEL BALL RUN: JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure—a determined competitor whose journey across the American frontier is driven by recovery, rivalry, and the search for a new way forward.

The racer who keeps moving

Johnny begins the Steel Ball Run race searching for a reason to ride again. His focus, competitiveness, and willingness to learn make him one of the story’s most compelling characters.

His partnership with Gyro Zeppeli becomes the emotional engine of the journey, while the race pushes Johnny to confront his past and redefine what strength means.
