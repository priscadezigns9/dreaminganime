Black Clover

Asta

Asta is a Black Bulls squad member whose anti-magic sets him apart in a world where nearly everyone uses magic. His goal is to become the Wizard King, and his refusal to give up is central to his official character profile.

Quick answer

Who is Asta?

The official Black Clover anime website lists Asta as a member of the Black Bulls, with Anti-magic as his attribute. Although he has no magic of his own, he wields a Five-Leaf Clover Grimoire and a sword that can cut through or deflect magic.

Character file

A dream without magic

Asta dreams of becoming the Wizard King. The contrast between that ambition and his lack of magic is part of what defines his place in the series. The official profile also highlights his physical strength and a strong heart that refuses to give up.

Official character details

The official anime profile lists Asta’s squad, Anti-magic attribute, birthday, background, goal, Grimoire, and sword.
