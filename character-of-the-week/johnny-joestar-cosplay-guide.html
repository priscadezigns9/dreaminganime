Character of the Week · Cosplay Guide

JOHNNY JOESTAR

Build a bold, convention-ready Johnny Joestar look inspired by STEEL BALL RUN: JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure—with the riding style, blue palette, and determined attitude that make the character instantly recognizable.

THE LOOK

Blue riding outfit

Start with a fitted blue riding jacket and matching trousers. Choose comfortable stretch fabric so the costume remains practical for walking and posing.

Cap and details

Use Johnny’s distinctive blue cap as the focal point. Add clean star-inspired details and trim with fabric paint, appliqué, or embroidered patches.

Boots and gloves

Pair the outfit with sturdy riding-style boots and fitted gloves. Break in the boots before the convention and keep the silhouette streamlined.

Finishing attitude

Johnny’s look is completed by a focused expression, controlled poses, and a confident rider’s posture. Let the character’s determination do the rest.

PROP CHECKLIST

Steel Ball

Use a lightweight foam or convention-approved replica. Never bring a metal projectile or sharpened prop.

Travel kit

Pack safety pins, fashion tape, a sewing mini-kit, blister protection, water, and a phone charger.

Pose set

Prepare three poses: a determined forward stare, a low rider stance, and a close-up holding the Steel Ball.

Convention reminder: Check the event’s prop policy before attending. Keep props lightweight, visibly harmless, and easy to peace-bond or inspect.

PHOTO PROMPTS

Road to the finish

Use a long path, parking structure, or open outdoor location for a dramatic rider portrait.

Steel Ball close-up

Frame the hands, cap, and prop together for a strong character-detail shot.

Orange-hour hero shot

Warm sunset light works well with the blue costume and Dreaming Anime’s orange accent color.
