Character file

A court butterfly in unfamiliar wings

In the Kingdom of Ei, five clans present maidens as imperial consorts, and only one will become empress. Reirin is admired for her beauty and refinement, but the body-swap betrayal with Shu Keigetsu abruptly places her in the dungeon and under threat of execution.

Rather than surrender, Reirin meets the crisis with composure and determination. Her story turns on the difference between the role the court assigns her and the strength she discovers in a body that is no longer her own.
