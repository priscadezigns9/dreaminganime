Maomao

A practical guide to Maomao’s palace apothecary look, with a reference first approach, adaptable materials, and comfort notes for a full convention day.

Start with one reference

Choose the version you want to recreate

Maomao wears several outfits across the series. Before shopping or sewing, pick one clear official anime image of the palace servant look and keep it beside you while you build. The familiar version uses a layered blue green robe, darker edging, and the signature dark hairstyle with green tinted ends and twin buns.

Use the character guide and the anime itself as your final reference for exact colors, trim, and accessories. Product listings below are adaptable starting points, not confirmed official replicas.

Build notes

The apothecary silhouette

01 · Base layers

Robe and collar

Start with a comfortable blue green cross collar robe or a plain tunic and wide sleeve layer. Use breathable fabric, a secure inner fastening, and a hem that clears your shoes. Add the darker collar and trim only after checking your selected reference.

02 · Hair

Dark wig with green ends

Choose a long dark wig with subtle green tinted ends, then arrange it into Maomao’s familiar twin buns and face framing fringe. Use lightweight red ties or ornaments, attached with wig safe clips rather than pins near the scalp.

03 · Finishing touches

Simple red accents

Keep cords and small ornaments lightweight. Match the placement and shade to the same official reference, and avoid adding jewelry or props that do not appear in that version.

04 · Fit check

Comfort before detail

Try the full outfit sitting, walking, and raising your arms. Keep sleeves and hems clear of the floor, secure the wig, and make sure the buns do not press against your headrest or obstruct your view.

Some costumes on Amazon use different colorways or combine outfit versions. Check the photos, garment measurements, seller details, shipping, and return terms before ordering.

Convention ready

A quick comfort checklist

Pack spare hair ties, a small sewing kit, safety pins, water, and blister protection. Keep sharp pins covered, secure long sleeves and hems, and follow the venue’s prop rules. Test the costume for a few hours before the event so you can adjust heat, movement, and fit.
