Quick answer

Who is Talia Yang?

Talia Yang is one of the four characters at the center of Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2. Official cast material describes her as raised in corpo towers, hardened by Maelstrom, fiercely independent, and steeped in the gang’s hyper-violent, chrome-obsessed culture.

Character file

Between corpo towers and Maelstrom

The official introduction gives Talia a sharp contrast: she was raised in corporate towers, then hardened in Maelstrom’s world. It describes her as fiercely independent and deeply familiar with the gang’s hyper-violent, chrome-obsessed culture.

That profile is enough to make her stand out before the series arrives. It sets corporate life and street-level gang culture side by side without revealing what choices Talia makes between them.

Editorial reading

Independence in a city built on power

Talia’s background invites questions about identity and survival in Night City: what does it mean to be raised among corporate towers and then hardened by a gang culture built around chrome and violence? That contrast is an interpretation of her announced profile, not a claim about her full story.

Pre-release note: This profile sticks to confirmed promotional details. It does not speculate about Talia’s loyalties, relationships, choices, or fate.
