SAKAMOTO DAYS

Taro Sakamoto

Once known as the strongest hitman, Taro retired after falling in love with Aoi. He now runs Sakamoto Store with his family, but his instincts and fighting skill are still very much alive.

Quick answer

Who is Taro Sakamoto?

Taro Sakamoto is a retired hitman who now runs Sakamoto Store. He gave up his former life after falling in love with Aoi; their daughter Hana is also central to the family he protects. The official character profile says his strength remains, and that he fights with whatever objects are at hand instead of a signature weapon.

Official sources: Character profile · SAKAMOTO DAYS official website · Season 2 announcement · Netflix

Character file

From legendary hitman to shopkeeper

Taro’s peaceful routine at Sakamoto Store is a retirement he chose for love. His life with Aoi and Hana gives him a reason to keep danger away from the people around him; stepping away from assassination never meant losing the ability to defend them.

The joke and tension of the character sit side by side: a quiet neighborhood shopkeeper can still react with the precision of a top assassin, often turning ordinary nearby objects into improvised weapons.

Character reading

Why the quiet life matters

Taro’s family life is not a break from the story’s action; it is what gives his choices weight. The official profile emphasizes his love for Aoi and Hana, while the series premise keeps his former world close enough to threaten the ordinary life he built. That contrast is the character’s central appeal: formidable skill used in service of a deliberately peaceful home.

This is Dreaming Anime’s interpretation of the official character setup, not a claim about audience consensus.
