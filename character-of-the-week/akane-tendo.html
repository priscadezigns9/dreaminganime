Who is Akane Tendo?

Akane is the third daughter of the Tendo family and a first-year student at Furinkan High School. The official profile highlights her outstanding physical ability and martial-arts sense, as well as a kind-hearted side that occasionally comes through in her relationship with Ranma.

Character file

The Tendo Dojo’s determined daughter

Akane is the youngest of the three Tendo sisters and trains in martial arts at the family dojo. The official anime profile describes her as a first-year student at Furinkan High School with exceptional physical ability and fighting sense.

Her strength is only one part of the portrait: the official description also points to the kind-hearted side she sometimes shows, especially against the friction that defines her dynamic with Ranma.

A spirited dynamic

Akane and Ranma

The official introduction establishes the central setup: Ranma Saotome and Akane Tendo are betrothed to each other by their parents. Their relationship is full of friction, but the official character profile notes Akane’s occasionally kind-hearted side. That push and pull gives the comedy its spark without reducing Akane to a single trait.

Akane’s martial-arts talent, confidence, and determination make her a standout in the series’ lively cast. Her character page also lists her as the voice of Noriko Hidaka in the Japanese cast.

Quick answers

Akane Tendo FAQ

Who is Akane Tendo?

Akane Tendo is the third daughter of the Tendo family, a first-year student at Furinkan High School, and a martial artist with outstanding physical ability and fighting sense.

What is Akane's connection to Ranma?

Their parents arranged their engagement. The official series introduction describes them as betrothed, while noting that they often clash and that Akane sometimes shows a kind-hearted side.

Who voices Akane in the Ranma ½ anime?

The official Ranma ½ cast page lists Noriko Hidaka as Akane Tendo's Japanese voice actor.

Why was Akane selected as Character of the Week?

Anime Trending's Fall 2026 Week 5 female-character chart, dated November 10, 2026, lists Akane Tendo at the top.
